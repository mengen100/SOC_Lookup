# Search indexing follow-up: 16 November 2026

Search Console coverage through 13 November: 82 indexed, 11 discovered but not indexed, 25 crawled but not indexed, and 3 redirects. The Windows event index and Event 1102 now appear in the indexed report. The two 28-day periods (3-30 September and 2-29 October) show 311 to 105 impressions and 2 to 1 clicks. These small samples do not establish the cause of the change.

URL Inspection showed successful smartphone Googlebot fetches, crawling and indexing allowed, and the expected user-declared canonical on all four pilot pages. Recorded crawls: 4624 and 4771 on 26 September; 4768 and 5140 on 23 August. No Google-selected canonical was displayed. Only the 4768 inspection reported a sitemap processing error; this does not establish a sitewide problem. No new indexing request or sitemap resubmission was made.

## Pilot improvements

- [4624](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/previous-versions/windows/it-pro/windows-10/security/threat-protection/auditing/event-4624): distinguish a host logon from authentication attempts and correlate its target logon ID with share access on the same host.
- [4768](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/previous-versions/windows/it-pro/windows-10/security/threat-protection/auditing/event-4768) and [4771](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/previous-versions/windows/it-pro/windows-10/security/threat-protection/auditing/event-4771): distinguish successful TGT issuance, preauthentication failures, and destination-host logons. Use the native XML Status field rather than assuming display labels are field names.
- [5140](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/previous-versions/windows/it-pro/windows-10/security/threat-protection/auditing/event-5140) and [5145](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/previous-versions/windows/it-pro/windows-10/security/threat-protection/auditing/event-5145): separate share-level access, requested access rights, and proof of file changes. Link to service-installation evidence as a follow-on pivot.

Check whether Google has fetched the revised version before attributing any change to this pilot. Compare page-level impressions and visible queries after a full reporting window, and compare the four pilot pages with the other unchanged URLs in the crawled-not-indexed group. The [Google coverage status](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7440203#crawled) does not identify a ranking penalty or guarantee that edits will result in indexing.
